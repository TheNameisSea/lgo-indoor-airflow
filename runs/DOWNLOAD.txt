Training output goes here, one directory per run. Nothing to download unless you
want the trained checkpoints:

    (released after the review period)

Put each run in runs/<run_name>/ with all four of its files:
    args.json  best.pth  thermo_stats.pt  history.json
A model is rebuilt from its own args.json and normalised with its own
thermo_stats.pt; evaluation refuses to run without them.
